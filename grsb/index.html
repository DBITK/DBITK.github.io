Who We Are

A community of musicians, united by a love of performance

The GRSB is a diverse ensemble of volunteer auditioned musicians, men and women of all ages who want to keep playing their instruments long after high school or college.

Through artistically challenging concerts, education outreach, and a youth soloist scholarship, we champion instrumental music as a lifelong experience and enrich the cultural life of greater Grand Rapids.

Music Director

Barry D. Martin

Conductor and Music Director since the 2004-05 season, Barry Martin is Professor of Music and former Director of Bands at Grand Valley State University, and a member of the prestigious American Bandmasters Association.

In constant demand across the country as a conductor, clinician, and adjudicator, he leads the GRSB with a commitment to musical excellence year after year.

Support the Music

Help keep great music playing

As a community band, the GRSB is largely supported by gifts from its members and audience. Donate $25 to $99 for a thank-you gift of concert tickets, or $100 and up to receive season tickets. You can even sponsor the chair of your favorite musician.
