Youth Soloist Competition

Finding Michigan's finest high-school soloist

2027 Competition

Perform with the band and earn a scholarship

The Grand Rapids Symphonic Band again sponsors its annual Youth Soloist Competition to find the state's finest high-school wind or percussion soloist. The opportunity is open to any student currently enrolled in a secondary band program in the State of Michigan.

The Award

$2,000 Scholarship

In addition to performing a solo with the band, the winning student receives a $2,000 scholarship, usable toward college, music camps, private lessons, instruments, and more. The youth soloist appears in concert with the GRSB, conducted by Barry Martin, in 2027 at Grandville High School (date coming soon).
