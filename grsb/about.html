About the Band

Premier adult community band · Founded 1978

Our Mission

Fostering excellence in performance

The Grand Rapids Symphonic Band is a premier adult community band that fosters excellence in performance, supports instrumental music as a lifelong experience, and contributes to the enrichment of cultural life in the greater Grand Rapids area.

This mission incorporates four objectives

  1. To provide artistically challenging and innovative band concerts.
  2. To give our audiences, sponsors, and members experiences with the highest quality wind band music across a wide range of styles.
  3. To support instrumental music education and lifelong music learning through clinics, conferences, solo competition, and outreach concerts.
  4. To serve as an exemplary model in fostering the growth and development of community bands.

The Musicians

A diverse group of dedicated volunteers

The GRSB recently performs five concerts each season at the Grandville High School Auditorium, where audience attendance has grown each year and now surpasses pre-COVID numbers. Historically, the band presented five to six concerts annually in Grand Rapids and surrounding areas, with outreach concerts in Muskegon, Rockford, South Haven, Hudsonville, Coopersville, Allendale, and Cedar Springs.

The band is a diverse group of volunteer auditioned musicians, men and women of all ages who want to continue playing their instruments after high school or college. Members donate considerable time and effort throughout the year: individual practice, weekly group rehearsals, and performances all place demands on already busy schedules. They value lifelong learning in music and contributing to the continuing music education of area high school students and the public. The love of music, and of performing difficult repertoire at an extremely artistic level, is why the GRSB began, and why it continues to flourish.

To further its mission of supporting music education, the GRSB offers free admission to students and a reduced-price ticket for an accompanying adult. The band also manages an annual Youth Soloist Competition for high-school students, with a scholarship award toward advanced music study. An annual side-by-side rehearsal enables advanced students recommended by local band directors to experience playing in advanced bands. Every two years, the band holds a Member Soloist Competition in which the winning member performs a solo with the band.
