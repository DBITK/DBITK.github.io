Support the Band

Help keep great music playing in West Michigan

As a community band, the GRSB is largely supported by gifts from its members and the community. Every contribution helps us continue performing great concerts and supporting music education.

Donate $25–$99 and receive a thank-you gift of concert tickets. Donate $100 or more and receive season tickets. You can also choose to sponsor the chair of your favorite GRSB musician. For details, contact Business Manager Susan Wygmans at info@grsymphonicband.org.
