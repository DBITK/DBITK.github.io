What to expect

You will be required to perform a solo of your own choosing in addition to sight-reading. No accompaniment is necessary. You must make an appointment for an audition, and please bring a copy of your piece for the conductor.

Auditions may also be available on an individual basis during our concert season.

To schedule: contact Ruth Wilson, Personnel Manager, at grsymphonicband@gmail.com or call 616-336-1554.
